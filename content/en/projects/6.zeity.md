## zeity

Zeity is a modern time tracking app focused on delivering an exceptional user experience. Perfect for freelancers and small teams who want to track their work hours efficiently. ⏱️

**Tech Stack:**
- Nuxt 3 for a fast, modern web app
- Vue.js for reactive UI components
- Open source and self-hostable
