## TripManager

An intuitive web app for planning and organizing your trips. Visualize your route on an interactive map and keep track of all your destinations! 🗺️

**Features:**

- Manage multiple trips simultaneously
- Add places and points of interest
- Interactive map view of your entire route
- Schedule dates for each location
- Perfect for road trips and round trips
