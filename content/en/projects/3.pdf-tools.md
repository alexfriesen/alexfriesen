## PDF Tools

A handy web-based tool for editing PDF files that runs entirely in your browser. No server uploads needed, all operations are performed locally!

**Features:**
- Merge and combine PDFs
- Reorder pages freely
- Delete unwanted pages
- 100% client-side, privacy-friendly
